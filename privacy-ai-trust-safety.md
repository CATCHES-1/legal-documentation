# Our Position on Privacy, AI, and Trust and Safety

**CATCHES**

Our Position on Privacy, AI, and Trust and Safety

Our technology operates at the intersection of AI and consumer data. That combination demands more than mere baseline compliance. This position statement outlines our approach to privacy, AI, security, and trust, and is intended for brand partners, investors, and other stakeholders seeking to understand our services and how we protect our users.

**Data Privacy**

**What privacy laws does CATCHES comply with?**

Our privacy program is built to satisfy the most restrictive applicable requirements across all jurisdictions in which we operate, including the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), and analogous frameworks across the world. We maintain a data subject request pipeline capable of meeting the applicable response deadlines across jurisdictions, conduct data protection impact assessments, and publish both a Privacy Policy and a standalone Biometric Notice.

**What data does CATCHES collect?**

When a user interacts with our virtual try-on (VTO) widget, we may collect user photographs, body measurements, and technical information, such as IP addresses and session identifiers. From uploaded photographs, our algorithm derives body geometry, physical characteristics, and body composition measurements to generate a personalized avatar.

**How does CATCHES handle biometric data?**

We treat all data derived from user photographs as biometric data, regardless of how it is classified under applicable law, because biometric data is subject to heightened protections.  We only collect it with affirmative consent from the user, encrypt the data in transit and at rest, never selling or sharing (as those terms are defined under applicable law), and deleted in accordance with strict, published retention schedules. We publish a standalone Biometric Notice alongside our Privacy Policy that details our collection, purpose, retention, destruction, and disclosure practices for biometric data.

**How does CATCHES obtain consent?**

Before we process any user-upload photograph (which we treat as biometric data), we present a clear disclosure to users that explain the data we derive from it, as well as how we use it and retain it. This complies with some of the strictest applicable consent requirements globally, such as those under BIPA and GDPR.

**Does CATCHES sell or share personal data?**

We do not sell or share personal data, as those terms are defined under applicable law, such as the CCPA. When a user opts into cross-brand personalization through their account, we use their data to power recommendations across participating brand storefronts, but all data remains on CATCHES’ infrastructure. No individual-level personal information is disclosed to brands. Users may opt out of cross-brand personalization at any time.

**AI Practices**

**How does CATCHES use AI?**

We use generative AI and physics-based simulation to create photorealistic virtual try-on experiences. Our AI analyzes user-uploaded photographs to generate a digital twin, a personal avatar, that closely simulates how garments fit, drape, and move on the user’s body. We also use AI-based content moderation filters to help identify inappropriate content and flag photographs that may depict minors.

**What does CATCHES *not* use AI for?**

We do not use AI for biometric identification or authentication, facial recognition, profiling for advertising or eligibility determinations, or any automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Virtual try-on results are simulations, not guarantees, and we disclaim fit accuracy accordingly.

**How are CATCHES’ AI models trained?**

Our models are trained exclusively on synthetic data and brand-provided product assets. We do not train on user photographs, body measurements, or biometric data. We may use VTO outputs (our own generated images) to evaluate and improve output quality, such as by flagging inaccurate results for correction. When outputs are used for this purpose, user data is anonymized.

**How does CATCHES approach AI regulation?**

We comply with existing AI regulatory requirements and actively monitor emerging frameworks across all jurisdictions in which we operate. Under the EU AI Act and similar regulations, our VTO engine does not constitute a high-risk AI system, as it does not perform biometric identification or make consequential decisions about individuals. We comply with the Act’s transparency obligations by disclosing AI processing in our Privacy Policy, Biometric Notice, and user consent flow. As new requirements take effect globally, we have a process to evaluate applicability to our technology and respond to it accordingly.

**Security**

**How does CATCHES protect user data?**

We operate on bare-metal infrastructure with customer and user data isolation and no shared tenancy. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Internal access is governed by single sign-on, multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access controls, and endpoint security. We conduct regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing and align our security practices with ISO 27001/27002 and the Cyber Essentials framework. Sub-processors are contractually bound to obligations at least as protective as our own and are subject to ongoing review.

**What happens in the event of a data breach?**

CATCHES maintains incident response procedures in accordance with the requirements of all applicable jurisdictions.

**Trust and Safety**

**How does CATCHES protect children?**

Our Service is restricted to users aged 18 and older. We enforce this through four layers of protection: 1) checkbox self-certification at the point of photograph upload; 2) AI-based photograph assessment that flags images that appear to depict minors; 3) if a photograph is flagged, the user is blocked from uploading photographs for that session; and 4) if a flagged user wants access to photograph-based features, they must create an account with email verification, which provides an additional friction layer and an auditable record.

**How does CATCHES handle content moderation?**

Our AI-based input filters screen all uploads before processing, rejecting inappropriate, non-human, and potentially minor-depicting images. No user-generated content is shared publicly or disclosed to brand partners.
