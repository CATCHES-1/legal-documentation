# CATCHES User Privacy Policy

*Last updated: April 2026*

CATCHES Limited (company number 10804799) (“CATCHES,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the CATCHES virtual try-on platform and related applications (collectively, the “Service”), including the CATCHES Virtual Try-On widget (the “VTO Widget”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect Personal Information when:

- Merchants install or integrate our Service;
- Users (“you”) interact with the VTO Widget on merchant storefronts;
- You create a CATCHES account; or
- You otherwise access or interact with our websites or Services.

CATCHES’ role under data protection law depends on the nature of the brand relationship. In some cases, CATCHES acts as an independent data controller (for example, when you create a CATCHES account or interact with the Service on a merchant’s storefront where CATCHES determines the means and purposes of processing). In other cases, CATCHES acts as a data processor on behalf of the merchant, processing your data solely on the merchant’s instructions. Where CATCHES acts as a processor, the merchant is the data controller and its privacy policy governs the processing of your data. This Privacy Policy applies to processing for which CATCHES is the data controller.

As used in this Privacy Policy, “Personal Information” means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with an identified or identifiable individual.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at: [privacy@catches.ai](mailto:privacy@catches.ai).

## 1. Information We Collect

When a merchant installs or integrates the Service, the applicable e-commerce platform (e.g., Shopify, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Magento, BigCommerce, or other supported platforms) provides us with access to certain store and product data. We collect only product-related information, such as:

We do not collect financial information, customer lists, or sensitive merchant data through these integrations.

### 1.2 Information We Collect Directly From Merchants

Merchants may provide additional information or upload assets directly into the Service, including additional product imagery, custom garment data, fit model or avatar information, and VTO Widget display settings or customization parameters.

We generate automated logs relating to merchant usage of the Service. These logs contain only anonymous interaction data (features accessed, buttons clicked, error events, and basic operational telemetry). No personally identifiable merchant customer data is included in these logs.

#### *(a) Account Information*

If you create a CATCHES account, we collect your name, email address, and password. You may also choose to provide additional profile information, such as display preferences. Creating an account enables persistent storage of your digital likeness and cross-brand personalization.

#### *(b) User-Submitted Photographs*

If a User uploads a photograph, it is used to generate a virtual try-on result. Before uploading a photograph, you will be presented with a disclosure describing the data CATCHES derives from your image. You must affirmatively consent to the processing of your biometric data and certify that you are 18 years of age or older before the upload proceeds.

By uploading a photograph, you represent and warrant that the photograph is of you and that you have the right to provide it to us. You may not upload photographs of other individuals without their consent. CATCHES reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate this requirement.

If you do not have a CATCHES account, your photograph and all data derived from it are deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service.

#### *(c) Body Measurements*

You may provide body measurement information to generate personalized virtual try-on results. This includes a size selection and, optionally, additional measurements such as height, chest, waist, hip, and inseam. Body measurements provided without a photograph are used to generate size-based try-on results. For Users without an account, this data is deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service.

#### *(d) Biometric Data*

Our VTO Widget may derive body geometry, physical characteristics, and body composition measurements from your submitted photograph to generate an accurate digital representation of your body for garment fit simulation. This information may constitute biometric data under applicable laws. We collect and process biometric data only with your explicit, informed consent, obtained before processing begins. For Users without an account, all data derived from your photograph is deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service. For detailed information about CATCHES’ biometric data practices, including purpose of collection, retention and destruction schedule, disclosure restrictions, and storage and protection standards, please see our Biometric Notice.

### 1.4 VTO Engine Processing

Our VTO engine processes User-uploaded imagery, body measurements, clothing assets and reference images, product metadata, and variant and SKU mappings. Users may select an avatar appearance or, if a photograph is uploaded, the system may automatically select the avatar that best corresponds to the image.

The VTO engine uses artificial intelligence, including generative AI models and physics-based simulation, to produce virtual try-on results. These results are AI-generated simulations and should not be relied upon as guarantees of actual garment fit.

We perform automated input monitoring and safety checking on uploaded photographs to ensure content integrity and prevent misuse. No data is stored as part of this monitoring process.

All processing occurs on secure, encrypted servers hosted on CATCHES-operated infrastructure and supported hosting environments. Both environments implement strict access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, and comply with applicable data protection requirements.

### 2.1 Provide and Operate the Service

To perform our contract with you, or where it is in our legitimate business interests to do so, we use Personal Information to:

- Operate, provide, and maintain the Service, including creating and managing your account;
- Generate virtual try-on results and fit simulations using the information you provide;

### 2.2 Improve and Monitor the Service

It is in our legitimate interests to improve and maintain the security of the Service, including protecting against security threats, fraud, or other malicious activities. We perform automated input monitoring and safety checking on uploaded content to ensure content integrity and prevent misuse. No Personal Information is retained as part of this monitoring.

### 2.3 AI Model Training and Improvement

We may use VTO outputs to evaluate and improve the quality of our AI models, such as by flagging inaccurate results for correction. When VTO outputs are used for this purpose, they are stripped of account identifiers and used only to improve output quality. We do not use your photographs, body measurements, or biometric data for model training.

### 2.4 Digital Likeness and Cross-Brand Personalization

If you create a CATCHES account, your digital likeness (the virtual representation of your body generated through the Service) may be stored in your account to enable you to use the Service across multiple merchant storefronts without re-uploading your photograph or re-entering your measurements. We may also use your interaction data and preferences to provide personalized recommendations, styling suggestions, or other features across participating brands within the CATCHES platform.

We may share aggregated, de-identified analytics with merchant partners (e.g., sizing trends, engagement data). We do not share your photographs, body measurements, or biometric data across brands or with merchant partners unless you separately consent. Cross-brand personalization is optional and is not a condition of using the Service. You may opt out of cross-brand personalization at any time by contacting privacy@catches.ai or, where available, through your account settings.

### 2.5 Analytics and Performance Monitoring

We collect aggregated and de-identified data (e.g., how many Users use a specific feature) to understand service performance and improve product functionality. We may use this anonymous data and share it with third parties for our lawful business purposes, including to analyze and improve the Service.

### 2.6 Compliance and Protection

We may use Personal Information to comply with applicable laws and legal processes; protect the rights, privacy, safety, or property of CATCHES and others; audit our internal processes for compliance; enforce our terms of service; and prevent, identify, investigate, and deter fraudulent or harmful activity.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain Personal Information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, in accordance with our retention policies and applicable laws, or until you withdraw your consent (where applicable). The following retention periods apply:

- **Account Information.** Retained for the duration of your account. Upon account deletion, your account information is deleted or anonymized within 30 days, except as required by law.
- **User Photographs.** For account holders, photographs are retained for the duration of the account. For Users without an account, photographs are deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service.
- **Body Measurements.** For account holders, retained for the duration of the account. For Users without an account, body measurements are deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service.
- **Biometric Data.** Retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, and in no event longer than 3 years. For Users without an account, biometric data is deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service. Biometric data will be permanently destroyed upon satisfaction of purpose or expiration of the retention period.
- **Non-Account Users.** Photographs, body measurements, and biometric data are deleted 30 days after your last interaction with the Service. Technical and debugging data is subject to the same 30-day retention period. De-identified usage data (stripped of all identifiers) may be retained for analytics.
- **VTO Outputs and Logs.** Cached briefly to improve performance. Technical logs retained for diagnostic and security purposes, then deleted or anonymized.
