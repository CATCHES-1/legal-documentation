# CATCHES User Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 October 2026** · Version 2.0

CATCHES Limited, company number 10804799, registered in England and Wales ("CATCHES", "we", "our" or "us"), operates the CATCHES virtual try-on platform and related applications (the "Service"), including the CATCHES Virtual Try-On widget (the "VTO Widget") that appears on the websites of fashion brands ("merchants").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you:

- use the VTO Widget on a merchant's website;
- create a CATCHES account;
- visit catches.ai or contact us; or
- work for a merchant or appear as a model in materials a merchant gives us.

"Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to or could reasonably be linked to you.

## Who is responsible for your information

**When you use the VTO Widget or a CATCHES account, CATCHES is the controller of the information you give us.** This includes your photographs, measurements, the data we derive from them, the try-on images we generate ("Visuals") and your account. We decide how this information is used. The merchant does not receive it unless you choose to share it with them.

**The merchant is the controller of information about your visit to its website**, such as the product page you viewed and orders you placed. The merchant shares some of this with us so the VTO Widget works and so it can pay us (see section 1.3). For that information we act on the merchant's behalf as its service provider, and the merchant's privacy policy also applies.

If you are in the UK or EEA, the merchant and CATCHES may be jointly responsible for collecting information through the VTO Widget on the merchant's website. We have agreed with each merchant who does what: we present the notices and consents in the VTO Widget and handle your requests, and the merchant describes the VTO Widget and its cookies in its own notices. You may contact either of us.

### 1.3 Information we receive from merchants

- **Product information**: product details, images and garment data.
- **Order and returns data**: for orders that carry the CATCHES Identifier, the order ID, date, value, currency, discounts, items, quantities and returns. We do not receive your name, address or payment details. We use this only to calculate fees, report to the merchant and measure the Service, and keep it pseudonymised for 24 months.
- **Comparison testing**: merchants may let us show the VTO Widget to some visitors and not others, to measure its effect. If you are in a comparison group, we record only that you were in it.

### 1.4 Brand partner personnel and models

We hold business contact details of people at merchants and suppliers to run our contracts. We also hold photographs and likenesses of models that merchants supply for product imagery. We use these only to make the images the merchant ordered, under the merchant's instructions and the model's consent, and delete them 12 months after the last image is delivered.

## 2. How we use information

### 2.1 To provide the Service

We use your information to generate Visuals in the size and colour you choose, to run your account, to fix problems and to keep the Service secure. Visuals are AI-generated simulations. They are not photographs and do not guarantee how a garment will fit.

### 2.2 Safety checks

We automatically screen uploaded photos to block unsuitable content and photos that appear to show someone under 18. We keep no copy of the photo as a result of this screening.

### 2.3 AI models

**We do not use your photographs, measurements or biometric data to train, fine-tune or improve our AI models.**

We may review a sample of Visuals, with account identifiers removed, to find and fix errors in our outputs. Because a Visual may still show you, we treat it as personal information, keep these samples for no more than 90 days and do not use them to train models. You can object at any time (section 7).

We do not use your information to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

### 2.4 Cross-brand features (accounts only, optional)

If you create an account and turn on cross-brand personalisation, you can reuse your saved likeness and measurements at other brands that use CATCHES. We may also show you products from those brands, and from the brand where you signed up, on CATCHES apps and services. This is based on your consent. You can turn it off in your account settings or by emailing privacy@catches.ai. We never use your photo or Visuals to promote products to other people.

### 2.5 Legal and safety

We use information to comply with the law, enforce our [End User Terms](https://docs.catches.ai/end-user-terms), protect people and prevent fraud.

## 3. Who we share information with

- **Service providers**, such as hosting, compute, monitoring and email providers. They act on our instructions under written contracts and may not use your information for their own purposes or to train their own models. A list is available on request from privacy@catches.ai. We update that list at least 30 days before adding a provider that handles End User data.
- **Our affiliate** Catches Inc, which helps us run the Service and acts on our instructions.
- **Merchants.** We give the merchant aggregated, de-identified reports, such as sizing trends and engagement. We share your photos, measurements, biometric data or Visuals with a merchant only if you choose to share them in the widget.
- **Professional advisers, authorities, and buyers of our business**, where needed and allowed by law. A buyer must honour this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric data.

## 4. How long we keep information

The periods are in the tables in section 1 and in our Biometric Notice. When a period ends we delete the information or make it anonymous. Backups are overwritten within 35 days.

## 5. International transfers

We store End User information mainly in the United Kingdom. Our affiliate and some service providers process it in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we protect transfers with an adequacy decision (including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient is certified), the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguard.

## 6. Security

We protect personal information with measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest;
- role-based access, least privilege and multi-factor authentication for administrative access;
- separated processing environments for End User data;
- logging and monitoring of access;
- automated deletion in line with section 4;
- secure development and vulnerability management, with independent penetration testing at least once a year;
- a documented incident response process, and business continuity and backup arrangements.

If a security incident affects your information, we will tell you and the relevant regulator where the law requires it.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your information (including in a portable format), correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it. You can also object to our use based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out.

To use these rights, email privacy@catches.ai or use the privacy controls in the VTO Widget or your account. We may need to verify your identity. We reply within one month in the UK and EEA, and within 45 days in the US, extendable where the law allows. You may use an authorised agent. If we refuse a request, we will explain why and how to appeal. We decide appeals within 45 days.

If you ask a merchant to delete your try-on data, it will pass your request to us. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

## 9. Children

The Service is only for people aged 18 or over. You must confirm your age before uploading a photo, and we use automated checks to block photos that appear to show someone under 18. If a photo is blocked, photo features stay unavailable unless you pass an age check. If we learn we hold information about someone under 18, we delete it. Parents can contact privacy@catches.ai.
